How a session works

  1. Check what they know. Play a short foundation game to see which ideas are secure and which need teaching.Play a game first.
  2. Pick a lesson. Filter by Primary, Junior Cycle, Senior Cycle or LCA. Pre-teach the vocabulary listed.Choose a lesson. Learn the words first.
  3. Practise on the board. Switch to learner view so only the pictures show. Tap any picture to hear it.Use the pictures together.
  4. Link to home. Share the home-link idea with parents, and point them to the free parents' course.Tell home what you learned.

A free, self-paced course for parents and caregivers of autistic children and young people. Short modules, plain language, and one small thing to try each week.
