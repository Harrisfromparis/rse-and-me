Relationships and sexuality education

RSE & Me

Relationships and sexuality education resources from Autism & Me. Clear, visual resources to help autistic young people and adults learn about bodies, boundaries, friendships, relationships and staying safe.

Each resource is built on Universal Design for Learning, so the same idea comes in more than one way: pictures, plain words, step-by-step routines and something to practise. We start from the person, not the curriculum, and we work with parents, carers and teachers as partners.

The toolkit supports SPHE and RSE in Irish schools at primary, Junior Cycle and Senior Cycle, including LCA. Most resources are free to download.

How our resources are made

  • Picture-firstEvery key idea has a picture. Body diagrams use correct names, so nothing is left vague or shameful.
  • Plain, literal languageShort sentences. No idioms or hints to decode. An easy-read version of every summary.
  • Low-arousal designCalm colours, steady layouts and predictable structure, page after page.

Aligned with the Irish curriculum

Every resource is tagged to the NCCA curriculum stage it supports, and follows NCCA guidance on teaching RSE to children with special educational needs: pre-teach vocabulary, use correct names, teach in many ways, revisit often, and work in partnership with parents.

Primary

SPHE & RSE

Schools teach the 1999 SPHE curriculum, supported by the NCCA's online Primary SPHE Toolkit. SPHE moves into the new Wellbeing area under the 2023 Primary Curriculum Framework.

Junior Cycle

SPHE (2023)

Updated specification taught from first year in September 2023, with 100 hours across three years. RSE is a key part of it.

Senior Cycle

SPHE & LCA

New Senior Cycle SPHE published October 2024, in place for fifth years by September 2027. In LCA it replaces Social Education Modules I and II from 2025.

Adults

Life beyond school

Resources for autistic adults, families and support staff, built on the same picture-first approach.
